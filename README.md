# 🛍️ Technology & Clothes Store Sales & Performance Analytics

A comprehensive, multi-page business intelligence and data analysis project using **T-SQL (SQL Server)** and **Power BI** to evaluate multi-year retail performance, customer demographics, and product inventory insights[cite: 20, 21, 22, 23].

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Retail operations across multiple categories (Electronics and Clothing) generate complex transactional data[cite: 20]. This project investigates sales dynamics, financial metrics, payment preferences, regional distribution, and customer loyalty tiers across the years 2022 to 2024[cite: 20, 21, 22, 23]. The insights are visualized into an executive multi-page Power BI dashboard suite[cite: 20, 21, 22, 23].

---

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard Highlights
The interactive reporting suite (`Technology and Clothes Store 1-4.png`) delivers deep analytical coverage across four primary views:

1. **Sales Analytics & Financial Performance:**
   - **Core Financial KPIs:** Total Revenue ($45.35M), Total Net Profit (9.70M), Total Orders (12K), and Average Order Value ($3.78K)[cite: 20].
   - **Category & Payment Distribution:** Equal split between Electronics (50%) and Clothing (50%), alongside balanced payment method revenues (Vodafone Cash, Cash, Debit Card, InstaPay, Credit Card, and Fawry)[cite: 20].

2. **Customer Demographics & Behavior Analysis:**
   - **Customer Metrics:** 3,000 Total Customers, 2,243 Active Customers, and 2,243 New Customers[cite: 21].
   - **Regional & Tier Breakdown:** Greater Cairo leading regional revenue ($6.8M), followed by Nile Delta, Alexandria, and others, alongside customer distribution across loyalty tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)[cite: 21].

3. **Product Performance & Inventory Insights:**
   - **Inventory Metrics:** 345 Total Products and 43.3K Total Units Sold[cite: 22].
   - **Top Seller:** *Abaja | Black | L* identified as the top-selling product[cite: 22].

4. **Sales Trend & Performance:**
   - **Temporal Trends:** Multi-year revenue trajectory mapping performance fluctuations from 2022 ($15.25M) through 2023 ($14.52M) up to 2024 ($15.58M)[cite: 23].
